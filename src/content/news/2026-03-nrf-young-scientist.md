title: Selected for the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) Outstanding Young Scientist Grants
category: award

Prof. Gyeore Yun's research proposal, *"Reinforcement Learning-based Audio-Haptic Cross-modal Rendering: From Physical Interaction Inference to Human-in-the-Loop Generation"*, has been selected for the Outstanding Young Scientist Grants (우수신진연구) funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) from March 2026 to February 2029.
