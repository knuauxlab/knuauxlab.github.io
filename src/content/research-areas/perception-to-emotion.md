---
title: From Perception to Emotion
summary: Investigating how humans perceive and interpret sensory stimuli, and the mechanisms connecting tactile perception to emotional experiences.
order: 1
---

We study human psychophysics and cross-modal perception across auditory, visual, and tactile channels. By understanding perceptual thresholds, simultaneity, and affective responses across the body, we establish perceptual models that bridge physical tactile stimulation and rich emotional user experiences.

- **Cross-Modal Psychophysics**: Investigating audio-tactile and audiovisual-haptic simultaneity, pitch matching, and intensity perception across the human body.
- **Affective Haptics & Tactons**: Designing expressive vibrotactile patterns (e.g., wearable wrist haptics) to convey emotion, personality, and semantic nuances.
- **Whole-Body Tactile Perception**: Modeling spatial and temporal tactile illusions such as seamless phantom sensations and perceived intensity variations under contact force.
