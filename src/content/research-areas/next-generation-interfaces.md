---
title: Next-Generation Interfaces
summary: Designing human-computer and XR interfaces that enhance both immersion and manipulation performance.
order: 3
---

We design novel haptic hardware, wearable systems, and physiological sensing interfaces that expand how users interact with virtual environments and tangible objects.

- **VR/XR Haptic Systems**: Full-body and wearable haptic interfaces that deliver directional, semantic, and realistic physical feedback in virtual reality.
- **Physiological & Biosignal-Driven Interaction**: Utilizing EMG (electromyography) and biomechanical modeling to render self-induced virtual weight and force sensations during natural hand and arm movements.
- **Tangible & Surface Haptics**: Vibration-mediated object recognition and virtual surface rendering (stiffness, damping, and contact transients) for intuitive physical interactions.
